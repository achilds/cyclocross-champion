How to play

W / ↑
Pedal (or run when on foot)
Shift
Sprint: more speed, burns more energy
←→ / AD
Lean: leaning is how you turn. Match the green zone on the lean gauge in corners.
↓ / S
Brake (front and rear together)
Space
Jump off / back on the bike. Run over barriers and stairs.
B
Balance: mash it fast in sand, mud, gravel and roots
E
Eat a gel (energy)
Q
Drink from your bottle (water)
C
Cheer on a nearby rider: you both get a little energy
Esc
Pause
  • Run out of energy or water and you collapse and are out of the race. The bar flashes when it's getting low. Food and bottles are limited, so pick your moment.
  • Every corner needs the right amount of lean. Too upright or leaning too far and you'll slide out. Faster = more lean, so brake before sharp corners.
  • Off-camber slopes tilt the track sideways: they drain your balance and have less grip. Slow down and mash B.
  • Riding into another rider makes you crash and lose energy. If someone crashes in front of you, steer around them!
  • Riding into barriers or stairs = crash. Watch for the warnings at the top of the screen.
  • Steep hills can be faster to run up. Stall on a climb and you'll topple over.
  • Finish in the top 3 to unlock the next course.
